# Worked examples

Fourteen files, each walking through one realistic situation from start to finish: the task, the prompt, what the AI produced (abridged, with its errors left in), how the errors were found, and what the final result looked like. The AI outputs are reconstructed from typical behaviour of current assistants; the mistakes they contain are the kinds that appear every day.

Read them before the module exercises; the exercises ask you to repeat the routine on your own task.
