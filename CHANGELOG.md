# Changelog

## 1.1 - October 2026

- **Module 8: AI in science and engineering work** - derivations, calculations, code, data, literature and lab reports, with the check that catches the typical AI failure in each.
- **Worked examples** (`examples/worked-examples/`): fourteen annotated case studies with the AI's errors left in.
- **Prompt library** (`examples/prompts/`): annotated prompts for studying, writing, coding, data analysis, literature and engineering.
- **Exercises** (`exercises/`): a bank of forty graded exercises with marking points, self-check quizzes for every module, and instructor notes.
- **Templates** (`templates/`): PROCESS.md, AI disclosure statement, prompt log, verification log, delegation brief.
- **Scripts** (`examples/python/`): `data_explore.py`, `check_citations.py`, `test_ai_function.py` (no API key needed); `compare_answers.py`, `quiz_from_notes.py` (API); a lab dataset (`examples/data/packed_bed.csv`) and a prompt-injection demonstration file.
- **Glossary** and **FAQ**; thirty capstone ideas and a complete sample PROCESS.md.
- Every module gained a "Common mistakes" section and links to its worked example, exercises and quiz; the teaching guide gained a 12-week schedule, grading guidance and an academic-integrity section.

## 1.0 - October 2026

Initial release: seven modules, teaching guide, capstone brief, two code examples.
