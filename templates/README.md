# Templates

Copy these into your own repository or notes and fill them in. They exist so that documenting AI use takes ten minutes, not an hour, and so that what you hand in shows what you checked.

| Template | Use it for | Modules |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md) | The process record required by the capstone and recommended for any AI-assisted assignment | all |
| [ai-disclosure.md](ai-disclosure.md) | The short statement that goes into a report, essay or submission | 6 |
| [prompt-log.md](prompt-log.md) | Recording prompts, what came back, and how you improved them | 1 |
| [verification-log.md](verification-log.md) | Recording claims, how you checked them, and the outcome | 2, 8 |
| [delegation-brief.md](delegation-brief.md) | Specifying a task for an AI agent: outcome, context, check | 4 |
