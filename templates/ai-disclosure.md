# AI disclosure statement (template)

Place a statement like this at the end of any submitted work where AI assisted, or where your course requires it. One paragraph is enough; specificity is what makes it credible.

## Template

> AI assistance was used in preparing this [report / essay / code]. Tool: [name and version; account type, e.g. "university Claude account", date]. Uses: [drafting the X section from my outline; writing and refactoring the analysis code; proof-reading; generating practice questions]. Checks: [all code tested against hand calculations; all references verified by DOI and read; figures compared with the raw data]. The [discussion / conclusions / design decisions / interpretation] are my own work. A full record of prompts and checks is in PROCESS.md.

## Examples

**Lab report (AI permitted):**

> AI assistance (university Claude account, 14 October 2026) was used to draft the analysis script from our test cases, to produce the figure code, and to proof-read the final text. Every computed value was checked against a hand calculation for one data point; all references were resolved and read. The discussion and conclusions were written by the group without AI. Prompts and checks are listed in PROCESS.md.

**Essay (AI permitted for feedback only):**

> I used an AI assistant (ChatGPT, October 2026) to obtain feedback on two drafts of this essay: it identified weak transitions and one unsupported claim, which I revised myself. No text in the essay was written by the AI.

**Code (AI permitted):**

> Approximately half of the functions in `analysis.py` were first drafted by GitHub Copilot and then edited; all were tested (see `tests/`). `plotting.py` is my own. The AI suggested the use of `scipy.optimize.curve_fit`, which I verified against the documentation.

**No AI used:**

> No AI tools were used in preparing this work.

## What makes a statement weak

- "AI was used for assistance." - which tool, for what?
- "AI was used for grammar" when it drafted sections.
- No mention of what was checked.
- A statement that transfers responsibility ("errors are due to the AI"). They are not.
