# Capstone Project: An AI-Assisted Tool for a Real Problem

Bring together everything from the course by building a small, real tool or resource with AI, and documenting how you worked.

## The brief

Choose a real problem from your studies, work, or community that AI could help with. Build a solution, publish it on GitHub, and reflect on the process.

Example ideas:

- A Python script that summarizes your weekly readings into study notes
- A small database of local resources with SQL queries that answer common questions
- A study guide for one of your courses, written in Markdown with AI-generated quiz questions you have verified
- A bilingual glossary of technical terms, checked by you for accuracy
- A non-technical option: a well-researched, fact-checked guide on a topic, with a full record of how AI was used

Thirty more ideas by field are in [project-ideas.md](project-ideas.md). A complete example of the process record is in [examples/sample-PROCESS.md](examples/sample-PROCESS.md).

## Requirements

Your GitHub repository must include:

1. **README.md** explaining the problem, your solution, and how to use it (module 7: Markdown).
2. **The work itself:** code, data, or documents.
3. **A commit history** showing how the project developed over time, with meaningful messages (module 7: Git).
4. **PROCESS.md** (template in [`templates/PROCESS.md`](../templates/PROCESS.md)) documenting:
   - Three prompts you used, and how you improved them (module 1)
   - At least three AI errors you caught and how you verified the output (module 2)
   - How you broke the project into tasks, and what you delegated vs. did yourself (module 4)
   - Any privacy or security decisions you made (module 6)
5. **A reflection** of 300 to 500 words: what did you learn, and what would you do differently?

## Assessment rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) |
|-----------|---------------|----------|----------------|---------------|
| **Problem and solution** | Real, well-defined problem; solution clearly works | Clear problem; solution mostly works | Problem vague or solution partial | Unclear problem or no working solution |
| **Communication** | Prompts show clear, deliberate improvement | Prompts are clear | Prompts are basic | Prompts are vague |
| **Verification** | Errors caught and verified rigorously | Some verification shown | Little verification | No verification |
| **Delegation** | Thoughtful division of labor, well justified | Reasonable division | Little planning | AI did everything without oversight |
| **Responsible use** | Privacy and security addressed explicitly | Mentioned | Barely considered | Not considered |
| **Documentation and Git** | Excellent README, clean history | Good README, regular commits | Basic README, few commits | Minimal documentation |
